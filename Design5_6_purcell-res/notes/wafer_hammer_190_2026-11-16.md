# Design5_6_purcell-res wafer hammer spacing and bare corners

The user confirmed that 190 µm is the centre-to-centre distance of the two
CPWs in the hammer region. A CPW has a 16 µm trace and two 9 µm etched
slots, so the clear distance between the facing etched edges is 156 µm.
The previous wafer geometry used 400 µm centres and a 366 µm clear distance.

Each of the six couplers has an 80 µm parallel section at 190 µm centre
spacing. The two lines move inward by 105 µm apiece and return to their
400 µm outer axes through tangent 99 µm radius bends. The end straight
segments are 5 µm. The changed region spans about 440 µm along the CPW.
Outside that region, the route axes and the channel meanders are retained.
The jogs add about 79 µm of physical centreline length to each CPW.

The local Q3D comparison uses the same four outer pin positions for the
original straight fixture and the new inset fixture. The first attempt
reported one grounded net because the curved fixture ends did not form
clean open terminations. The revised fixture uses straight ends and checks
for two fully isolated conductors before solving.

| Local Q3D case | CPW centre spacing (µm) | Head width × height (µm) | Head gap (µm) | Mutual capacitance (fF) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original straight fixture | 400 | 25 × 65 | 42 | 1.50888 |
| Inset with reference head height | 190 | 25 × 30 | 40 | 0.80299 |
| Selected inset | 190 | 25 × 54 | 40 | 1.50389 |

The selected heads start 50 µm from each inset CPW centre and end 75 µm from it.
The mutual capacitance is 0.33% below the baseline. The last two estimates differ
by 0.83% for the baseline and 0.17% for the selected case. Neither case reports
solver convergence. This is a local electrostatic check with common ground. Whole
chip coupling, linewidth and frequency are pending revalidation. HFSS was not rerun.
See the [Q3D summary](../results/resonator/20261116_160806_hammer_190_q3d_070bbb59/summary.json)
and [run manifest](../results/resonator/20261116_160806_hammer_190_q3d_070bbb59/run.json).

The wafer fab GDS uses positive metal on layer 1/0, a 10 × 10 mm outline,
and the shared ground perforation settings. Any perforation extending into
the 500 µm edge keep-out is filled. Layouts omit the four crosses, die ID
and corner test structures. The centred launch locations are retained.

The r4 inset shape is historical. The archived [r4 fab GDS](../gds/previous/Design5_2026-11-16_wafer_r4_fab.gds)
and [r4 source snapshot](../results/resonator/20261116_160806_hammer_190_q3d_070bbb59/code/)
record it. The current layout and export are described in the [r5 notes](wafer_hammer_parallel_2026-11-16.md).
