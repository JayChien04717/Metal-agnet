Changes w.r.t. Design1

 * FX flux line: U-loop height 80 um (was 50), tip gap 11 um (was 15) (MyFluxonium defaults).
 * Corner test structures drawn with TestJunction (geometry unchanged).

2026-11-12: capacitor stems added to the FX pads (30 um wide, 30 um gap between the stem ends;
MyFluxonium stem_width / stem_gap) and the simulation model is now FluxoniumArrayJJ (JJ = short gap in the
middle of the lead line, U = two lumped-L sides, 100 nH each, + metal
closing end). The numbers below are from BEFORE the stems: re-run Q3D.
