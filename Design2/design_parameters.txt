 * CPW 10 / 6 um (notebook: 15 / 5.909 um, flux line / tmon flux line gap 8.409 um) for the
   flux, charge and readout lines of the components (new component defaults).
 * The two small arm stubs on each fluxonium pad stem are not drawn (show_l_arms = False).

   JJ = short gap in the middle of the lead line (qubit centre), U = two lumped-L sides
   (100 nH each) + metal closing end (as Design1 / 3). Not yet
   used for the Q3D numbers below.
 * Junction-free (2026-11-12, build_design default jj_metal=False): no JJ pads between the Q1 / Q2
   stems, no SQUID pads on Q3. The Q3D run cpw10_6 below still had the JJ pads (not rerun).
 * Only Q1, Q2, Q3 are rendered (as in notebook cell 33), pins open-terminated:
   charge / flux / fake flux / readout lines of Q1, Q2 and the flux lines of Q3.
 * Q3D: Medium order, no auto increase, 0.5 %, 6 passes (same as Design1), 0.6 min.
 * Not rendered: resonators, feedline, other three FTF sets.
