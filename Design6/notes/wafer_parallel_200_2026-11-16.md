# Design6 wafer r6 parallel readout

The requested CPW center spacing is 200 µm on both readout channels, down from 400 µm in r4. The 16 µm traces and 9 µm slots give a 166 µm spacing between the facing etched edges. The left channel axes are at x = −1.500 and −1.300 mm. The right channel mirrors those coordinates. The filter axis and its feedline tee move toward the resonator by 200 µm. The resonator axes and CPW lengths are held to the explicitly selected [2026-11-14 length input](../parameters/Design6_2026-11-14.json). The filter and resonator lengths are 3.82263 and 4.03611 mm for Q1, and 3.94469 and 4.15878 mm for Q2.

The selected hammer uses a 25 × 37.5 µm head, a 40 µm face gap, a 16 µm ground clearance, and a 55 µm distance from the CPW center to the outer head face. A [local Q3D comparison](../results/resonator/20261116_164545_parallel_hammer_200_quick_e869cf3e/raw/q3d_summary.json) used identical axial limits in both coupons at the mean common straight span of the two channels. Mutual capacitance was 1.61301 fF at 400 µm and 1.64708 fF at 200 µm (+2.11%). Neither extraction reached the requested convergence flag in eight passes. The last mutual-capacitance changes were 0.60% and 0.68%, respectively. The coupling check covers the local mutual capacitance. It omits the outer meanders, ground straps, and distributed loading of the full readout.

The user accepted the +2.11% change. The [run record](../results/resonator/20261116_164545_parallel_hammer_200_quick_e869cf3e/run.json) captures both compared geometries and the simulation inputs. The [r6 fab mask](../gds/previous/Design6_2026-11-16_wafer_r6_fab.gds) passed [direct GDS readback](../results/resonator/20261116_164545_parallel_hammer_200_quick_e869cf3e/raw/gds_readback.json) across six slices per CPW. The [400 µm parent snapshot](../results/resonator/20261116_164545_parallel_hammer_200_quick_e869cf3e/inputs/2/Design6_2026-11-16_wafer_r4_fab.gds) and the [superseded 190 µm candidate](../gds/previous/Design6_2026-11-16_wafer_r5_fab.gds) are archived. The [current r7 fab mask](../gds/Design6_2026-11-16_wafer_r7_fab.gds) retains the r6 readout and coupon geometry and fills ground perforations within the fabrication keep-outs.

The user accepted the Q1 frequency shifts and requested the Fab GDS update. The [Q1 HFSS comparison](../results/resonator/20261116_164545_parallel_hammer_200_quick_e869cf3e/summary.json) used two adaptive passes with a 50 µm length mesh constraint on metal, with shifts of +123 MHz for the narrow, resonator-like mode (6.83835 to 6.96105 GHz) and +170 MHz for the broad, Purcell-filter-like mode (6.90543 to 7.07522 GHz). Assignment follows linewidth. The maximum adaptive frequency changes were 7.94% and 5.21% across the three solved modes for the reference and r6, respectively. Convergence and full-chip validation are pending. Q2 was stopped after the user accepted the Q1 result. The delivered die contains four transmon test junctions with T labels.
